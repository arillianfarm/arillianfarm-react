Arillian Farm

An Eggcellent Place To Be

Welcome friend! If you’re into farmy, DIY, quirkiness, then you're in the right spot!

Arillian = Arthur + Jillian (A mashup of my boyfriend’s and my names).
Arillian Farm is the (self-labeled) micro-farm in our backyard… which is to say, we have a bunch of raised garden beds, a small flock of chickens, a couple (wether) goats, three dogs and a lil pond with a few hundred mosquito fish on about a third of an acre in Mesa, AZ.

Our Backyard is my home-gym, salad bar, animal sanctuary and Personal HG TV Channel

When it’s too hot to tinker around outdoors, I spend a lot of time writing humorous and/or thought-provoking booksand blog entries. Recently, I've Been Cooking Like Crazy and ' cataloging new and favorite recipes.

Arthur is my high-tech, hottie, handyman, hero.

He’s an electrical engineer who loves to “work on things” –particularly cars, audio and lighting and especially audio and lighting for cars.

Sometimes Arthur and I work on DIY projects together - Like transforming a tough shed into a divided goat and chicken barn, building a catio at my rental property in Tempe, or making a little fishpond / chicken wading pool for our chickens (and sometimes I do some crafting with trash and upcycle empty LaCroix boxes into planters and egg cartons).

If you’ve enjoyed wandering through our little corner of the web and want to help support my lumber and hardware habit, click a couple bucks our way on our Go Fund Me, Buy some Merch or tell a friend or check out videos of our dog pack: Pajama Dog, Huey Ka-Bluey and Xena Dog, goats: Totes and McGoats and Broodzilla, Scout, Aya and the rest of Our Colorful Chicken Flock on our You Tube!
